    Week of Sept 13¶

    • Started watching the show The Pitt. I love a good medical drama and this one is great.

    • Caught up with a lot of friends. I spoke to varsha and suraj after like ages. Suraj is coming to India. I recently learnt that Suraj's younger brother has moved to Bangalore. He has charged me to "choote so smabhal ke rakhna", something which I take very seriously.

    • Need to catch up with other friends too. I've been lagging on my socials for a while. I have been feeling exceedingly exhausted after social so Ive been avoiding those.

After a while the kiddo got bored and we proceeded to install the sticks upright on a patch of mud. After that he gave me a stone to take home. I gave secured it carefully in my cupboard.

    • I am planning to get tested for ADHD.

    • I have started reading the book Games; Art as Agency. It is quite a read.

    Running and lifestyle¶

    This week I ran 42k. This was fully inspired by Kevins partner. She is an incredible runner and upon hearing my goal of running an ultra (30k) in Nov, she started giving me a hard time about not having enough running volume.

    She is obviously right. I need to step up my game.

    Now I am considering to seriously follow a 14-day training block. I was trying to cram all types of workouts in a 7 day block which was not easy. Thinking is 14-day blocks really helps me pace myself. Thus I have more energy to live a normal life and my workouts are also of good quality.

    I also decided that for zone-2 specifically I can and should cross-train. Specifically the rowing machine. I am in-luck because the gym closest to my home does have one.

    I am also slightly disappointed about the time this will realistically take. Realistically to get better at running I will need atleast 12 to 18 months of consistent training. My dream of running the 65k Buddha Trails is not wise. I will not sign up for it. Instead I will focus on getting my regular training. I hope I am in a better shape next year.

    I am hoping to improve my current z2 page which sits at about 10 mins per km with a z2 hr of 140. I am hoping the pace gets better.

    As it stands now, most of my weekends will be devoted to running / training. I am unable to engage is social activities on weekends because of this.

    I have also started to sleep early and wake up early. This makes evening plans very delicate. It is a change, but I am sure that there is no other way. I need to focus on what is really important.

    Diet¶

    My Diet over the last week has been great. I have been eating the same meals consistently.

    3 eggs for breakfast, 1 dosa for lunch, 1 chai + egg puff in the evening and an open Shawarma for dinner.

    7 days, same meal. It was great. I like not having to think about food this way.
    Saturday was weird because for some reason the egg-puffs in all the stores near my house ran out. Maybe the Chickens are on a strike ? I am not sure as I am not following the daily chicken news.

    I really like having a routine. It helps me think about other things.
